ABOUT US

Engineers Without Borders (EWB) is a global network of non-profit organizations that unite engineers, professionals, and students to address critical challenges. They apply engineering expertise to improve living conditions in underserved communities.

MISSION
  • Enable SVCE students to apply engineering knowledge to real-life problems in a practical and sustainable way.
  • Build a student-led space where collaboration, innovation, and responsibility grow naturally.
  • Promote hands-on engineering that balances technical skills, ethics, and community impact.
VISION

To be a student chapter recognized for impactful innovation, nurturing socially conscious engineers, and building lasting connections between technology and communities for sustainable change.
