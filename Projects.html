Currently Ongoing Projects

Smart Ambulance Traffic Control (AUTOGREEN)

An EWB project designed to give ambulances a clear path by turning red lights green when they approach. The system reduces delays, speeds up emergency response, and helps save lives, with future scope in IoT and AI-based traffic optimization.

Microplastic Remedation

Our research addresses LDPE microplastic pollution using an integrated biodegradation approach that merges fungal degradation with supplementary biological agents. This synergistic method enhances plastic breakdown efficiency, showing clear improvements in surface modification and mass reduction. It presents a sustainable, eco-friendly pathway for microplastic remediation and future large-scale environmental applications.

Projects Completed

Coastel Cleanup

EWB-SVCE students took part in a coastal cleanup drive at Marina Beach, promoting environmental awareness and community responsibility. The event highlighted the importance of protecting our shoreline and marine ecosystem.

Think Out Loud

EWB-SVCE launched the Think Out Loud initiative to encourage students to express ideas, enhance critical thinking, and develop creative problem-solving skills. Through digital participation via Google Forms, the program promoted structured reasoning, active engagement, and innovative perspectives among students.

Community Project

The Community Outreach Program (COP’25) at Government C.M.S. Higher Secondary School, Kanchipuram, inspired students from 6th to 12th standard through live science demonstrations and career guidance. By combining interactive sessions with sapling plantations and cleanliness drives, the initiative successfully fostered scientific curiosity and environmental responsibility, leaving a lasting educational impact on the community.

Carbon Foot Print

EWB-SVCE conducted a Carbon Footprint Awareness Initiative to promote responsible energy usage and environmental sustainability. The initiative encouraged students to adopt simple energy-saving habits, demonstrating how small actions like switching off unused appliances can significantly reduce carbon emissions and contribute to a greener campus

Field Visit

EWB-SVCE students participated in a field visit to Space Zone India, gaining hands-on exposure to rocket propulsion, satellite technology, and aerospace engineering. The visit enhanced practical learning through expert interactions, laboratory sessions, and insights into real-world space technology applications.
